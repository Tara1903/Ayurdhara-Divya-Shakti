# Design Quality Checklist

Before finalizing any visual asset, the design must pass this strict quality gate:

- [ ] **Luxury Perception:** Does this look luxurious? Does it look like a ₹2,000 premium wellness product?
- **Restraint:** Are we relying on elegant typography and generous whitespace rather than clutter and complex graphics?
- [ ] **Alignment & Grid:** Is everything perfectly aligned? Are the margins consistent and generous?
- [ ] **Color Accuracy:** Are we using only the approved brand colors (Cream, Primary Green, Gold Accent)? Are there any neons or oversaturated colors? (If yes, reject).
- [ ] **Typography Check:** Are we strictly using Cinzel (Headings), Montserrat (Subheadings), and Poppins (Body)? Are we using a maximum of two font families per asset?
- [ ] **Botanical Integrity:** If herbs are shown, are they scientifically accurate line art or premium watercolor? (Reject any clipart or cartoon graphics).
- [ ] **Material Realism (for Mockups):** Does the packaging look like real amber glass and matte labels under professional studio lighting?
- [ ] **Brand Consistency:** Would Apple approve this level of design discipline? Would Aesop sell this? Does this immediately build trust?
- [ ] **Print Readiness:** If for print, is it 300 DPI, CMYK, with correct bleeds? Are QR and barcodes high contrast?

*If the answer to any question is "No", redesign it before presenting it.*
