# Packaging Rules

## Front Label Layout
The front label is strictly for brand recognition and primary product identification. 
- **Top:** Logo Mark (Gold Embossed)
- **Upper-Mid:** Brand Name (Cinzel Bold)
- **Mid:** Product Name (Cinzel Bold)
- **Lower-Mid:** Variant / Target (e.g., "Kids", "Men") in Montserrat SemiBold
- **Bottom:** Volume (e.g., 10 ml) and "External Use Only" (if applicable) in Poppins
- **Graphics:** Subtle botanical line-art fading into the background, or positioned elegantly at the bottom/top borders.

## Back Label Layout
The back label handles all regulatory and informational density without cluttering the aesthetic.
- **Left Column / Upper Half:** Ingredients list and Directions for Use.
- **Right Column / Lower Half:** Warnings, Batch Number, Mfg Date, Expiry, MRP, Customer Care details.
- **Bottom Edge:** QR Code (Left) and Barcode (Right) clearly printed in black for scannability.

## Outer Box Rules
- Material is always high-grade 300–350 GSM White Duplex Board with Premium Matte Lamination.
- The front mirrors the bottle's minimal front label.
- Avoid large blocky colored backgrounds; rely on the Cream base with elegant typography and subtle botanical engravings.

## Dimensions Compliance
- **10ml Oil:** 38x45mm Label
- **20ml Oil:** 45x55mm Label
- **Boxes:** Follow exact dielines (e.g., 4x4x2 inches for Trial Box).
