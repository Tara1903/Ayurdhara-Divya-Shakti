# SECTION 1
# PURPOSE

This document establishes the Quality Control (QC) and Brand Compliance framework governing every commercial asset created for AYURDHARA DIVYA SHAKTI.
Its purpose is to ensure that every asset released by the Creative Operating System satisfies commercial, manufacturing, marketing, digital, and brand standards before approval.

Quality Control is not responsible for generating creative work.
Quality Control is responsible for protecting the brand from incorrect creative work.
No commercial asset may bypass this document.

---

# SECTION 2
# QUALITY PHILOSOPHY

Quality is defined by correctness rather than appearance.
An asset shall not be approved because it appears attractive.
An asset shall be approved only when it accurately represents:
• The Brand
• The Product
• Ayurveda
• Manufacturing Reality
• Website Consistency
• Marketing Objectives
• Customer Trust

Visual beauty without accuracy is considered failure.
Commercial accuracy with timeless design is considered success.

---

# SECTION 3
# QUALITY CONTROL AUTHORITY

The Quality Control Department possesses final approval authority over every commercial asset.
Its authority extends to:
Packaging
Photography
Marketing
Website Assets
Presentation Boards
Advertising
Product Renders
Educational Graphics
Catalogues
Print Material
Social Media

Quality Control may reject any asset regardless of previous approvals.
Only the Brand Owner may override Quality Control.

# SECTION 4
# QUALITY REVIEW PIPELINE

Every completed asset shall pass the following review stages.

Stage 1: Technical Review
↓
Stage 2: Brand Review
↓
Stage 3: Product Review
↓
Stage 4: Packaging Review
↓
Stage 5: Photography Review
↓
Stage 6: Website Review
↓
Stage 7: Marketing Review
↓
Stage 8: Manufacturing Review
↓
Stage 9: Legal Review
↓
Stage 10: Final Approval

Failure at any stage immediately returns the asset for revision.

# SECTION 5
# TECHNICAL REVIEW

The Technical Review verifies production quality.

Checklist:
□ High Resolution
□ Proper Aspect Ratio
□ Sharp Typography
□ No Pixelation
□ No Distortion
□ Correct Alignment
□ Correct Margins
□ Balanced Composition
□ Proper Contrast
□ Export Quality
□ Print Ready
□ Digital Ready

Technical defects automatically fail Quality Control.

---

# SECTION 6
# BRAND REVIEW

The Brand Review verifies that the asset protects the AYURDHARA DIVYA SHAKTI identity.

□ Official Logo
□ Approved Typography
□ Approved Colour System
□ Approved Brand Personality
□ Approved Tone
□ Premium Positioning

# SECTION 14
# CRITICAL FAILURES

The following issues result in automatic rejection.

• Wrong Brand Name
• Wrong Product Name
• Fake Ingredients
• Placeholder Text
• Hallucinated Information
• Perfume Appearance
• Cosmetic Appearance
• Incorrect Product Category
• Wrong Packaging
• Incorrect Typography
• Wrong Colours
• Poor Print Quality
• Manufacturing Impossibility
• Website Incompatibility
• Generic Luxury Style
• Trend-Based Design

Critical failures bypass scoring and immediately return the asset for correction.

---

# SECTION 15
# QUALITY SCORE

Every approved asset shall receive an internal quality score.

Scoring:
95–100 : Commercial Masterpiece
90–94 : Premium Commercial Ready
85–89 : Approved
80–84 : Minor Revision Required
70–79 : Major Revision Required
Below 70 : Reject

Only assets scoring **90 or above** shall be considered flagship-quality brand assets.

---

# SECTION 16
# FINAL APPROVAL

Approval shall only occur when all departments return APPROVED status.

Required approvals:
□ Brand Compliance
□ Product
□ Packaging
□ Photography
□ Website
□ Marketing
□ Manufacturing
□ Legal
□ Quality Control

If any department returns REJECTED, the asset shall not be released.
