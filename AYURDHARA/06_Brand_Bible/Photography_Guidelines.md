# Photography & Illustration Guidelines

## Product Photography
Every generated image must resemble a professional commercial studio shoot.
- **Lighting:** Natural soft lighting, diffused, no harsh shadows.
- **Reflections:** Controlled reflections on the amber glass and gold embossing.
- **Shadows:** Studio-quality drop shadows anchoring the product to the surface.
- **Styling:** Minimal props. Focus purely on the elegant product. If ingredients are shown, they must be raw, high-quality, and placed meticulously.
- **Depth of Field:** Natural lens blur on background elements (bokeh) to draw focus to the label and bottle.
- **Materials:** Showcase realistic textures—the matte feel of the label, the glossy ribbed cap, the thick amber glass.

## Botanical Illustrations
When botanical ingredients are represented graphically on packaging or marketing material:
- **Style:** Scientifically accurate botanical line art.
- **Aesthetic:** Premium engraving style or soft, minimal watercolor.
- **Color:** Kept to monochromatic (Primary Green) or subtle desaturated tones.
- **Prohibitions:** Absolutely NO cartoon herbs, NO generic vector clipart, NO overly vibrant/saturated plant illustrations.
