# Purpose

ADS-PACKAGING-SYSTEM defines the complete packaging philosophy, structure, hierarchy, material standards, print requirements, label architecture, and manufacturing guidelines for every AYURDHARA DIVYA SHAKTI product.

This document serves as the master authority for all physical product packaging.

# Packaging Hierarchy

Every package shall follow the same visual hierarchy.

1. Brand Logo
2. Brand Name
3. Product Category
4. Product Name
5. Variant (if applicable)
6. Key Usage Information
7. Net Quantity
8. Regulatory Information

# Governing Rule

ADS-PACKAGING-SYSTEM governs every physical package, label, bottle, carton, shipping box, and printed packaging asset produced for AYURDHARA DIVYA SHAKTI.

In case of conflict:

ADS-CONSTITUTION-000 takes precedence.
ADS-BRAND-BIBLE governs brand identity.
ADS-PRODUCT-INTELLIGENCE governs product names.
ADS-COLOR-018 governs colours.
ADS-TYPE-019 governs typography.
ADS-PRINT-017 governs print production.

Every packaging asset must comply with this document.
