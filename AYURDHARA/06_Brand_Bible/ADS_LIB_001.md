# SECTION 1
# MATERIAL LIBRARY

Purpose
Materials communicate quality before customers read a single word.
Incorrect materials immediately reduce perceived product value.
Every render shall use approved material references.

1.1 Amber Pharmaceutical Glass
Status: PRIMARY MATERIAL
Description: Deep amber pharmaceutical glass with realistic wall thickness, subtle transparency, accurate light absorption, smooth curved edges, premium manufacturing precision, no plastic appearance, no excessive tinting.
Psychology Communicates: Trust, Scientific precision, Ayurveda, Premium quality, Product protection.
Approved Uses: Bottles, Dropper bottles, Tonic bottles.
Never Use: Bright orange glass, Brown plastic, Cartoon transparency, Unrealistically black glass.

1.2 Matte Paper Label
Appearance: Premium matte coated stock with soft light diffusion.
Characteristics: Minimal glare, High readability, Smooth finish, Sharp typography, Luxury perception.
Avoid: Glossy sticker appearance, Plastic label, Wrinkled paper, Over-textured paper.

1.3 Gold Foil
Purpose: Premium emphasis. Not decoration.
Approved Uses: Logo accents, Collection titles, Limited editions, Borders (minimal).
Maximum Coverage: Less than 5% of visible label area. Gold shall support hierarchy. Never dominate hierarchy.

1.4 Soft Touch Finish
Perception: Luxury, Warmth, Craftsmanship, Modern premium wellness.
Preferred For: Gift boxes, Premium collections, Presentation packaging.

1.5 Embossing
Purpose: Create tactile hierarchy.
Approved: Logo, Brand seal, Premium collection name.
Never emboss large body text.

# SECTION 2
# SURFACE LIBRARY

Purpose
The supporting surface should elevate the product without competing with it.

2.1 Travertine Stone
Priority: ★★★★★
Emotion: Calm, Architectural, Premium, Natural, Modern.
Applications: Website Hero, Luxury Photography, Packaging Showcase, Presentation Boards.
Colour: Warm cream.
Texture: Subtle. Never highly patterned.

2.2 Limestone
Emotion: Soft, Refined, Clean, Scientific.
Applications: Clinical wellness, Modern Ayurveda, Educational visuals.

2.3 Oak Wood
Emotion: Warm, Craftsmanship, Traditional wellness, Premium authenticity.
Grain: Straight, Minimal knots, Muted finish.

2.4 Linen Fabric
Purpose: Soft background element. Never become primary subject.
Characteristics: Natural folds, Cream tones, Low contrast.

2.5 Handmade Paper
Use only for: Traditional storytelling, Educational cards, Ayurvedic history.
Never as the primary packaging material unless specifically approved.

# SECTION 3
# LIGHTING LIBRARY

Philosophy
Lighting defines emotional perception.
Every lighting style shall have a business purpose.

Museum Light
Priority: ★★★★★
Emotion: Timeless, Premium, Elegant.
Characteristics: Soft shadows, Controlled highlights, Excellent material definition. Perfect for hero photography.

North Window Light
Emotion: Authentic, Honest, Calm.
Characteristics: Soft directionality, Neutral colour, Natural falloff. Ideal for product pages.

Commercial Softbox
Emotion: Precision, Professionalism, Manufacturing quality.
Use for: Packaging validation, Catalogues, E-commerce.

Morning Sun
Emotion: Wellness, Routine, Freshness, Hope.
Use sparingly. Never create harsh shadows.

# SECTION 5
# BACKGROUND LIBRARY

Approved colours:
Warm Cream, Soft Beige, Muted Stone, Ivory, Light Sand, Natural White.

Approved environments:
Premium wellness clinic, Minimal luxury interior, Architectural stone, Japanese minimalism, Modern Ayurveda studio, Muted natural environments.

Prohibited:

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

# SECTION 6
# BOTANICAL LIBRARY

Botanicals shall be used with restraint.

Approved:
Single fresh leaf, Small branch, Root section, Flower only if relevant, Seeds, Natural bark, Fresh herbs.

Never create botanical walls or jungles.
The product remains the hero.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

# SECTION 7
# LUXURY DETAILS

Luxury is communicated through restraint.

Approved cues:
Controlled reflections, Soft shadows, Natural imperfections, High-quality materials, Accurate scale, Fine typography, Precise spacing, Quiet backgrounds, Negative space.

Forbidden cues:
Gold everywhere, Floating particles, Smoke, Sparkles, Liquid explosions, Heavy bokeh, Oversaturated colours, Excessive props.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

# SECTION 8
# AYURVEDA VISUAL DNA

Every render shall communicate:
Modern Ayurveda, Scientific trust, Traditional wisdom, Clean manufacturing, Natural ingredients, Premium wellness, Minimal luxury, Quiet confidence.

The visual language shall never resemble:
Luxury perfume, Cosmetics, Alcohol, Fashion accessories, Fantasy herbal products, Religious posters, Generic stock wellness imagery.

Floating bottles, Broken reflections, Plastic glass, Unreadable labels, Hallucinated logos, Incorrect logo hierarchy, Brand name above logo, Brand name separated from logo, Crowded compositions, Artificial HDR, Lens flare, Magic particles, Random butterflies, Excessive flowers, Overgrown leaves, Floating ingredients, Impossible shadows, Incorrect bottle proportions, Oversized caps, Fake embossing, Fake foil, Placeholder text, Lorem Ipsum.
