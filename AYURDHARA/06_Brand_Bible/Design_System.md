# AYURDHARA DIVYA SHAKTI: Design System

This system is the foundational blueprint for all AYURDHARA visual assets. It ensures absolute consistency, scalable to 500+ products, maintaining a luxury wellness aesthetic at every touchpoint.

## 1. Core Principles
- **Luxury through Restraint:** Never crowd the design. Let whitespace do the heavy lifting.
- **Hierarchy:** Primary information first (Brand, Name, Volume). Secondary information goes to the back.
- **Consistency:** Every variant must feel like a sibling, not a distant cousin.
- **Authenticity:** Only use scientifically accurate botanical line-art, no generic clipart.

## 2. Grid & Alignment
- **Centering:** All central label text is center-aligned to create a traditional, apothecary-style symmetry.
- **Margins:** A strict minimum 15% safe margin around all active content on any printed surface (labels, boxes).
- **Proportions:** Utilize the Golden Ratio (1:1.618) when determining the relationship between the logo size and product title size.

## 3. Typography Architecture
- **Logo / Brand Mark:** Cinzel Bold
- **Product Title:** Cinzel Bold (Capitalized)
- **Sub-titles / Variants:** Montserrat SemiBold (Tracking +100)
- **Body / Specifications:** Poppins Regular (Tracking 0)

## 4. Color System Implementation
- **Base Background:** Always Cream (`#FFFBEA`) for physical packaging and main backgrounds.
- **Primary Text:** Dark Charcoal (`#2D2D2D`) for maximum readability.
- **Accents:** 
  - Primary Green (`#2E7D32`) for botanical elements and secondary accents.
  - Premium Gold (`#C9A227`) for premium touches (embossed logo, fine borders).
- **Variant Color Coding:** Applied as a subtle accent bar or in the botanical illustration color:
  - Kids: Sage Green
  - Men: Navy Blue
  - Women: Elegant Rose
  - Senior: Walnut Brown

## 5. Material & Finish Code
- **Glass:** Amber Brown only. No clear or frosted glass unless a specific premium tier dictates it.
- **Plastic/Caps:** Matte Black ribbed for standard oils. 
- **Labels:** Matte finish PP, waterproof. No gloss.

## 6. Expansion Rules (Scaling to 500 Products)
- **New Categories:** When introducing a new category (e.g., Skincare, Supplements), maintain the exact same grid, typography, and logo placement. The only acceptable variance is the form factor of the container (e.g., jar vs. bottle) and a designated variant color if applicable.
- **Naming Convention:** `[Category/Target] [Primary Benefit/Ingredient] [Form]`. (e.g., *Kids Smart Oil Blend*, *Feet Wellness Oil*). Keep names minimal.
