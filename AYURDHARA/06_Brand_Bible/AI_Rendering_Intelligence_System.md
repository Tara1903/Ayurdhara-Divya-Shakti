# PART 1
# PHILOSOPHY OF PROMPT ENGINEERING

This document defines how the Creative System interprets language to generate agency-level commercial imagery.
It does not teach "how to write prompts." It defines how the AI must *think* about prompting.

**Core Principles of AI Interpretation:**
- **Prompt Order Matters:** The rendering engine prioritizes the beginning of a prompt. Brand and Product must precede lighting and environment.
- **Concept Dominance:** Strong visual keywords will overwrite subtle ones if not carefully balanced.
- **Token Hierarchy & Semantic Priority:** Language must be structured hierarchically. The engine must explicitly define what is primary, secondary, and tertiary.
- **Hidden Ambiguity:** The system must remove all ambiguity. "Beautiful" is ambiguous. "Balanced composition with soft window lighting" is precise.
- **Visual Inference & Hallucination Prevention:** The engine must explicitly lock down background and materials to prevent the model from hallucinating unwanted elements.
- **Prompt Saturation:** Overloading a prompt with too many adjectives dilutes the core subject. Prompts must remain disciplined.

# PART 4
# VISUAL VOCABULARY DATABASE

The system shall translate subjective adjectives into objective rendering instructions.

**"Luxury" means:**
Balanced composition, negative space, premium materials (amber glass, travertine), soft gradients.

**"Premium" means:**
High contrast, sharp typography, flawless reflections, disciplined lighting.

**"Modern" means:**
Clean lines, geometric stability, minimal props, stark backgrounds.

**"Scientific" means:**
Clinical lighting, macro focus, high sharpness, neutral colour temperature, zero clutter.

**"Organic" means:**
Soft shadows, natural botanical integration, warm colour tones, linen/wood textures.

**"Warm" means:**
4800K–5600K lighting, amber highlights, morning light.

**"Elegant" means:**
Tall aspect ratios, ample vertical whitespace, thin typography weights, muted secondary colours.

# PART 5
# CAMERA INTELLIGENCE

Camera settings dictate psychological perception.

**Lenses:**
- **35mm:** Environmental context. Use for lifestyle, showing the product within a wellness space.
- **50mm:** The human eye. Use for honest, undistorted product portraits.
- **85mm:** Professional studio portraiture. Use for Hero shots requiring slight compression and subject isolation.
- **100mm Macro:** Texture and detail. Use for label close-ups, oil viscosity shots, and material craftsmanship.
- **135mm:** High compression. Use for flattening backgrounds and isolating the product in luxury compositions.

**Angles:**
- **Eye Level:** Trust and equality.
- **10°–15° (Slightly Above):** Clear view of product top/cap while maintaining label readability.
- **Straight Front:** E-commerce standard, clinical, precise.
- **Perspective Correction:** Vertical lines must remain perfectly vertical (architectural tilt-shift rules).

**Depth of Field:**
- **Focus Stacking:** Infinite depth of field for technical Hero shots where every detail must be sharp.
- **Shallow Bokeh:** For lifestyle or mood shots to blur backgrounds and force focus on the hero ingredient or logo.

# PART 6
# LIGHTING INTELLIGENCE

Lighting is the primary driver of emotional tone.

**Approved Profiles:**
- **Morning Light:** Warm, directional, long soft shadows. Communicates beginnings and natural wellness.
- **Window Light:** Soft, diffused, natural gradient across the product. Communicates authentic lifestyle.
- **North Light:** Cool, consistent, shadowless. Communicates clinical precision.
- **Studio Softbox:** Perfect, controlled gradients on glass. Communicates premium manufacturing.
- **Rim Lighting:** Edge highlights to separate the amber glass from dark backgrounds.

**Micro-Lighting Control:**
- **Specular Response:** Must perfectly map to the curvature of the amber bottle.
- **Ambient Fill:** Shadows must never be pure black; they must retain ambient room colour (e.g., warm grey).
- **Glass Lighting:** Light must pass *through* the amber glass, illuminating the oil inside, not just reflecting off the surface.

---

# PART 7
# MATERIAL RENDERING INTELLIGENCE

The system must instruct the model on how materials behave, not just what they are.

- **Amber Glass:** High Index of Refraction (IOR). Must show volumetric absorption, appearing darker at the edges and glowing warmly in the centre where light passes through.
- **Matte Paper (Label):** High surface roughness. Zero specular highlight. Must absorb light softly.
- **Gold Foil:** Anisotropic reflection. Must catch light sharply at specific angles. Never a flat yellow colour.
- **Oil:** Golden tone, high viscosity. Light passing through must scatter warmly (subsurface scattering).
- **Travertine/Stone:** Micro-porous texture. Soft, diffused ambient occlusion in the crevices.
- **Cotton/Linen:** Woven normal map. Must show micro-shadows along the threads.

---

# PART 8
# COMPOSITION INTELLIGENCE

- **Golden Ratio & Rule of Thirds:** Used to place the product focal point (e.g., the logo).
- **Negative Space:** Essential for luxury. The product should occupy no more than 40-50% of the frame in Hero shots to allow visual breathing room.
- **Visual Weight:** Botanicals or props on the left must be balanced by shadow, text, or negative space on the right.
- **Eye Flow:** Lighting gradients and leading lines must guide the eye straight to the brand name.

---

# PART 9
# PRODUCT RENDERING INTELLIGENCE

Every physical product component has rules.
- **Bottle:** Perfect symmetry. No warped geometry.
- **Label:** Must adhere to the grid. Must wrap perfectly around the cylinder.
- **Cap:** Must show realistic threading or closure gaps. Cannot melt into the bottle.
- **Contents:** The oil level must be perfectly horizontal regardless of camera angle.

---

# PART 10
# AI MODEL INTELLIGENCE

The prompt architecture must adapt to the specific rendering engine being used (e.g., Midjourney, Flux, Ideogram, GPT-4o Image).

- **GPT/DALL-E:** Requires highly structured, explicit layout instructions. Avoids stylistic keyword stuffing. Excellent for mockups.
- **Midjourney:** Requires heavy negative prompting to control "hallucinated luxury" and over-dramatization. Excellent for atmospheric, photorealistic sets.
- **Flux:** Requires precise material and physics instructions. Excels at photorealism but needs rigid compositional constraints.
- **Ideogram:** Prioritized for exact typographic reproduction and label accuracy.

---

# PART 11
# NEGATIVE PROMPT INTELLIGENCE

Negative prompts shall be categorized to prevent engine loopholes.

- **Geometry Errors:** no warped bottles, no floating objects, no impossible perspective, no broken symmetry, no bent packaging.
- **Material Errors:** no plastic glass, no fake foil, no rubber reflections, no wet paper, no flat oil.
- **Brand Errors:** no perfume aesthetics, no cosmetic styling, no luxury alcohol bottles, no generic herbal cliches, no placeholder typography.
- **Photography Errors:** no overexposure, no HDR, no neon, no lens flare, no heavy smoke, no particle explosions, no CGI look, no oversaturation.

# PART 12
# PROMPT AUTO-CORRECTION ENGINE

Before any prompt is executed, the AI must internally verify:
1. Does this match the Brand Bible?
2. Does the packaging adhere to ADS-GRID-015?
3. Is the typography compliant with ADS-TYPE-019?
4. Is the lighting compliant with ADS-PHOTO-016?
5. Are the colours compliant with ADS-COLOR-018?
6. Does it pass the ADS-QA-010 Critical Failure checks?

If any answer is NO, the prompt must be rewritten before generation.

# PART 14
# SELF-CRITIQUE ENGINE

Upon generation, the asset must be evaluated against the 10-point scale.
- Packaging realism (10/10)
- Typography accuracy (10/10)
- Lighting physics (10/10)
- Brand consistency (10/10)
- Premium perception (10/10)
- Manufacturing realism (10/10)

If the overall score is < 90/100, the image is internally rejected and regenerated with refined prompts.
