# CASE STUDY 001
## Premium Website Hero

Project: Website Hero Banner
Business Objective: Create an immediately recognizable premium first impression for visitors. Increase perceived trust. Support premium pricing. Strengthen brand recall.
Customer Objective: Within three seconds the visitor should understand: Premium Ayurveda, Scientific trust, Modern wellness, High-quality products.

Creative Challenge: The hero image must: Show only one hero product. Leave sufficient negative space for website headlines. Remain responsive across desktop and mobile. Avoid visual clutter.

Documents Loaded:
✓ ADS-CONSTITUTION-000
✓ Brand Bible
✓ Typography System
✓ Colour System
✓ Photography Manual
✓ Render Intelligence
✓ Visual Knowledge Library
✓ Creative Vocabulary

Creative Reasoning: Instead of using decorative botanical scenes, select a restrained architectural setting. Choose travertine to communicate permanence and premium craftsmanship. Use museum-quality soft lighting to reveal material quality. Keep the bottle as the dominant visual element. Avoid excessive props.

Visual Specification:
Camera: 85mm, Eye Level
Composition: Bottle centered slightly right. Negative space on the left. Background blurred subtly.
Lighting: Large soft source. Soft rim highlight. Controlled reflections.
Materials: Amber glass, Matte paper, Minimal gold foil, Travertine.
Emotion: Quiet confidence. Modern Ayurveda. Scientific trust.

Execution Strategy: Generate hero image. Review label. Verify hierarchy. Adjust reflections. Confirm typography readability. Approve.

Quality Review:
Brand Consistency: 10/10
Packaging Accuracy: 10/10
Material Quality: 10/10
Commercial Readiness: 10/10

Common Mistakes: Too many botanicals. Floating bottle. Busy background. Logo too small. Brand name separated from logo. Harsh shadows. Overly cinematic lighting.

Lessons Learned: Less visual complexity increases premium perception. Negative space improves website usability. Soft lighting better communicates wellness than dramatic lighting.

# CASE STUDY 002
## Premium Packaging Mockup

Business Objective: Evaluate packaging before production.
Customer Objective: Not applicable. Internal review.

Creative Challenge: Create an image that accurately represents manufacturing quality. No marketing embellishments.

Documents Loaded: Packaging Manual, Print Production Manual, Colour System, Typography System, Constitution.

Creative Reasoning: Accuracy takes priority over aesthetics. Neutral studio lighting. Orthographic front view. Correct dimensions. Maximum label readability.

Visual Specification:
Background: Neutral white
Lighting: Commercial softbox
Camera: 50mm, Straight front
Shadow: Minimal
Purpose: Manufacturing approval

Common Mistakes: Perspective distortion. Incorrect amber glass. Fake foil. Incorrect embossing. Unreadable typography.

Lessons Learned: Manufacturing validation requires restraint. Beauty is secondary to accuracy.

# CASE STUDY 003
## Instagram Product Launch

Business Objective: Introduce a new product. Increase awareness.
Customer Objective: Understand: What the product is. Why it matters. Who it is for.

Creative Challenge: Communicate one message within three seconds.

Creative Reasoning: Single hero bottle. Soft botanical support. Minimal headline. Premium background. Strong visual hierarchy.

Visual Specification:
Platform: Instagram
Aspect Ratio: 4:5
Camera: 85mm
Lighting: Morning natural
Surface: Travertine
Typography: Minimal

Common Mistakes: Too much text. Multiple products. Crowded layout. Distracting props.

Lessons Learned: One product. One message. One emotion.
