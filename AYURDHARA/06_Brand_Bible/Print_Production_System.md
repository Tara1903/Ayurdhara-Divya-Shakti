# SECTION 1
# PURPOSE

Packaging is the customer's first physical interaction with the brand.
The purpose of this document is to establish manufacturing-quality standards for every physical packaging component produced under the AYURDHARA DIVYA SHAKTI Creative Operating System.

Packaging shall be designed for production first and aesthetics second.
Every visual decision shall remain manufacturable.

---

# SECTION 2
# PHYSICAL DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

Every package shall communicate quality before it is opened.
The customer shall subconsciously perceive:
Precision
Professional Manufacturing
Premium Materials
Authenticity
Cleanliness
Trust
Modern Ayurveda

The customer should immediately believe:
"This company invests in quality."

# SECTION 3
# PACKAGING MATERIAL LANGUAGE

Amber Glass
Matte Waterproof PP Labels
Premium Duplex Board
Rigid Box Board

Matte Lamination
Selective Spot UV

# SECTION 4
# BOTTLE STANDARDS

Every bottle shall appear:
Balanced
Stable
Premium
Comfortable to hold
Manufacturable

Bottle proportions shall remain consistent across the product family.
Bottle realism shall include:
Correct Wall Thickness
Natural Glass Refraction
Real Cap Threads
Accurate Neck Dimensions
Proper Oil Fill Level
Visible Glass Weight

---

# SECTION 5
# LABEL STANDARDS

Every label shall communicate professionalism.
The label shall:
Wrap naturally
Maintain straight alignment
Follow bottle curvature
Avoid unrealistic stretching
Avoid floating edges
Typography shall remain readable after curvature.

---

# SECTION 6
# PRINT QUALITY

Print shall appear commercially manufactured.

Characteristics:
Sharp Text
Rich Blacks
Controlled Ink Density
Smooth Gradients
Accurate Registration
Consistent Colour
No banding
No pixelation
No fuzzy typography
No jagged edges

Every printed surface shall resemble offset or high-quality digital commercial printing.

---

# SECTION 7
# MATTE VS GLOSS

Default Finish: Matte

Reasons:
Premium perception
Reduced reflections
Better readability
Luxury appearance
Soft tactile feel

Gloss shall only be used intentionally.
Examples:
Spot UV
Protective coating
Highlighting logos
Accent graphics

Gloss shall never dominate the package.

---

# SECTION 8
# FOIL APPLICATION

Foil shall communicate craftsmanship.

Approved foil:
Gold
Rose Gold (Limited Editions)
Copper (Traditional Collections)

Foil shall emphasize:
Logo
Collection Name
Premium Seal
Special Edition

Foil shall never replace typography.
Foil shall support hierarchy.

---

# SECTION 9
# EMBOSS & DEBOSS

Embossing shall remain subtle.

Preferred usage:
Logo
Brand Seal
Premium Collection
Traditional Pattern

Excessive embossing reduces elegance.
Embossing shall create tactile value rather than decoration.
