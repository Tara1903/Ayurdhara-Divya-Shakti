# AYURDHARA DIVYA SHAKTI
# PRODUCT INFORMATION ARCHITECTURE & CONTENT SYSTEM
## Document ID: ADS-CONTENT-020
### Version: 1.0
### Status: PERMANENT
### Classification: INTERNAL

---

# SECTION 1
# PURPOSE

This document defines where every piece of product information belongs across the AYURDHARA DIVYA SHAKTI ecosystem.
Information shall be distributed intentionally.
No medium shall be overloaded.
Customers shall receive the right information at the right time.

---

# SECTION 2
# INFORMATION HIERARCHY

Every product shall communicate through four information layers.

Layer 1: Packaging
↓
Layer 2: Product Information Card
↓
Layer 3: Website Product Page
↓
Layer 4: Educational Knowledge Base

Each layer has a specific purpose.

---

# SECTION 3
# PACKAGING CONTENT

Packaging exists for recognition and purchase.
Packaging shall contain only essential information.

Approved content includes:
• Brand Name
• Product Name
• Product Category
• Net Quantity
• Primary Benefit
• Required Regulatory Information
• Batch Number
• Manufacturing Date
• Expiry Date
• Manufacturer Details
• QR Code

Packaging shall remain visually calm.
Long paragraphs are prohibited.

---

# SECTION 4
# PRODUCT INFORMATION CARD

Every premium product may include a printed information card.
The card provides a deeper understanding without cluttering the packaging.

Approved sections:
Welcome Message
Brand Philosophy
Product Overview
How to Use
Usage Frequency
Storage Instructions
Precautions
Customer Support
QR Code for More Information

The card shall educate rather than advertise.

---

# SECTION 5
# WEBSITE PRODUCT PAGE

The website is the primary source of detailed product information.

It may include:
Complete Product Description
Benefits
How to Use
Who It Is For
Frequently Asked Questions
Usage Routine
Storage Guidance
Lifestyle Recommendations
Related Products
Customer Reviews
Downloadable Product Guide

The website shall be the authoritative reference for customers.

---

# SECTION 6
# EDUCATIONAL KNOWLEDGE BASE

Educational content shall exist independently from product marketing.

Topics may include:
Ayurvedic Wellness Principles
Lifestyle Practices
Seasonal Self-Care
Daily Rituals
Ingredient Education
General Wellness Guidance

This section builds long-term trust through education.

---

# SECTION 7
# QR CODE STRATEGY

Every QR code shall lead customers to value.

Approved destinations include:
Official Product Page
Product Guide
Usage Videos
Support Centre
Authenticity Verification

The QR code shall never redirect to unrelated promotional pages.

---

# SECTION 8
# WRITING PRINCIPLES

Every piece of content shall be:
Clear
Honest
Respectful
Simple
Professionally Written
Educational
Evidence-aware

The tone shall inspire confidence without exaggeration.

---

# SECTION 9
# CONTENT CONSISTENCY

The same product shall communicate consistently across:
Packaging
Information Card
Website
Brochure
Social Media
Customer Support

Differences in wording are acceptable.
Differences in factual information are not.

---

# SECTION 10
# FINAL PRINCIPLE

Good packaging creates curiosity.
Good documentation creates understanding.
Good education creates trust.

Long-term trust creates lasting customer relationships.
