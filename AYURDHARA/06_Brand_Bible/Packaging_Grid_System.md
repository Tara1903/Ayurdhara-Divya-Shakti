# AYURDHARA DIVYA SHAKTI
# PACKAGING GRID SYSTEM & TYPOGRAPHY MATHEMATICS
## Document ID: ADS-GRID-015
### Version: 1.0
### Status: PERMANENT
### Classification: INTERNAL

---

# SECTION 1
# PURPOSE

This document establishes the mathematical foundation of every packaging design produced for AYURDHARA DIVYA SHAKTI.
Packaging shall never be created by visual estimation.
Every margin, alignment, spacing, proportion and typography decision shall follow a structured grid system.

The objective is to create packaging that appears engineered rather than decorated.

---

# SECTION 2
# DESIGN PHILOSOPHY

Good packaging is invisible.
The customer should notice the product, not the designer.

The packaging system shall prioritize:
• Clarity
• Balance
• Precision
• Readability
• Consistency
• Manufacturability

The layout shall communicate confidence through structure.

---

# SECTION 3
# MASTER GRID

Every label shall begin with a modular grid.

Recommended grid:
12 Vertical Columns
16 Horizontal Rows
Uniform Margins
Consistent Gutters
Safe Print Area
Bleed Area

No object shall ignore the established grid.

---

# SECTION 4
# SAFE AREAS

Every label shall define four zones.

Bleed Area
↓
Trim Line
↓
Safe Area
↓
Content Area

Critical information shall remain inside the Safe Area.
No typography shall cross the trim line.

---

# SECTION 5
# MARGIN SYSTEM

Margins communicate premium quality.
Minimum outer margins shall remain visually balanced.

Top Margin
Bottom Margin
Left Margin
Right Margin

Margins shall never be adjusted independently without reason.
Consistent margins create visual stability.

---

# SECTION 6
# TYPOGRAPHY SCALE

Typography shall follow a predictable hierarchy.
Recommended order:

Level 1: Brand Name
↓
Level 2: Product Name
↓
Level 3: Product Category
↓
Level 4: Primary Benefit
↓
Level 5: Supporting Information
↓
Level 6: Ingredients
↓
Level 7: Usage Instructions
↓
Level 8: Legal Information

Every level shall maintain proportional scaling.
No level shall visually overpower higher-priority information.

---

# SECTION 7
# TYPOGRAPHY SPACING

Spacing shall remain consistent.
The system shall define:
Letter Spacing
Word Spacing
Line Height
Paragraph Spacing
Headline Gap
Section Gap

The customer should never perceive typography as crowded.

---

# SECTION 8
# VISUAL ALIGNMENT

Every object shall align with the grid.
Alignment shall occur using:
Left Alignment
Centre Alignment
Baseline Alignment
Column Alignment
Optical Alignment

Random positioning is prohibited.

---

# SECTION 9
# INFORMATION BLOCKS

Packaging shall be divided into logical information groups.
Brand Block
Product Block
Benefit Block
Ingredient Block
Usage Block
Certification Block
Legal Block
Manufacturing Block
Barcode Block
QR Block

Every block shall have consistent spacing.

---

# SECTION 10
# LOGO CLEAR SPACE

The brand logo shall always maintain protected space.
No text.
No illustrations.
No decorative elements.
No icons.
No borders.
may enter the protected logo area.

The logo must remain visually isolated.

---

# SECTION 11
# GOLDEN PROPORTIONS

Visual balance shall be based on proportion rather than decoration.
The Creative System may use proportional systems such as:
Golden Ratio
Rule of Thirds
Modular Scale
Baseline Grid

Only when they improve communication.
Mathematics shall support clarity.
Not replace it.

---

# SECTION 12
# NEGATIVE SPACE

Empty space is an active design element.
The Creative System shall intentionally preserve whitespace around:
Logo
Product Name
Bottle Neck
Hero Ingredient
Premium Finish
Major Headlines

Negative space improves:
Luxury Perception
Readability
Customer Confidence
Premium Positioning

---

# SECTION 13
# ICONOGRAPHY

Icons shall be functional.
Icons shall communicate information.
Decorative icons are prohibited.
Every icon shall have documented meaning.
Icon style shall remain consistent across all products.

---

# SECTION 14
# COLOUR BLOCKING

Colour shall organize information.
Colour shall never create confusion.

Colour blocks may distinguish:
Category
Benefits
Ingredients
Warnings
Usage
Promotional Information

Colour shall not compete with typography.

---

# SECTION 15
# LABEL BALANCE

Every label shall maintain visual equilibrium.

The visual weight of typography, graphics, colour, and whitespace shall remain balanced.
No side of the label shall appear visually heavier without purpose.

---

# SECTION 16
# PRINT MATHEMATICS

Every production file shall account for:
Bleed
Trim
Safe Zone
CMYK Conversion
Ink Density
Foil Tolerance
Emboss Tolerance
QR Readability
Barcode Readability
Manufacturing Tolerance

Packaging shall remain production-ready without redesign.

---

# SECTION 17
# TYPOGRAPHY MATHEMATICS

Typography shall follow mathematical relationships.

Recommended scaling systems include:
1 : 1.125
1 : 1.25
1 : 1.333

The chosen scale shall remain consistent throughout the product family.
Arbitrary font sizing is prohibited.

---

# SECTION 18
# OPTICAL CORRECTIONS

Mathematical perfection is not always visual perfection.

The Creative System may apply optical corrections for:
Logo Position
Headline Alignment
Bottle Curvature
Rounded Labels
Cap Alignment
Bottle Perspective

Optical corrections shall improve perceived balance while respecting the underlying grid.

---

# SECTION 19
# CONSISTENCY ENGINE

Every future product shall inherit the same grid architecture.
New products may introduce new content.
They shall not introduce new layout logic.
The grid system is permanent.

---

# SECTION 20
# FINAL PRINCIPLE

Customers rarely notice a grid.
They notice its absence.

The Packaging Grid System exists to ensure every AYURDHARA DIVYA SHAKTI product communicates precision, trust, and premium craftsmanship through invisible structural discipline.

Structure creates confidence.
Confidence builds trust.
Trust builds brands.
