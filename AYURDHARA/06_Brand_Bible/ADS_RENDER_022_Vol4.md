CHAPTER 1
MODEL ABSTRACTION LAYER
1.1 Purpose

The Creative System shall never become dependent upon a single AI image generation model.
Image generation technology evolves rapidly.

The creative intelligence of AYURDHARA DIVYA SHAKTI shall remain independent of any individual rendering engine.

The system shall therefore separate:
Business Intelligence
↓
Creative Intelligence
↓
Visual Intelligence
↓
Prompt Intelligence
↓
Model Translation
↓
Image Generation

Only the final layer changes when migrating to a new rendering model.
Everything above remains permanent.

1.2 Model Independence Principle

The Creative System shall define intent rather than syntax.

Incorrect approach:
Write a Midjourney prompt.

Correct approach:
Construct the visual specification.
Translate the specification into the syntax most appropriate for the selected rendering model.

The visual specification is permanent.
Prompt syntax is temporary.

CHAPTER 4
DYNAMIC PROMPT OPTIMIZATION

Before rendering, the system shall optimize the assembled prompt.

Checks include:
Duplicate concepts
Contradictory instructions
Redundant adjectives
Missing product information
Camera conflicts
Lighting conflicts
Material inconsistencies
Typography ambiguity
Background ambiguity
Rendering ambiguity

Only optimized prompts shall proceed to generation.

CHAPTER 5
NEGATIVE PROMPT INTELLIGENCE

Negative prompts shall not be generic.
Instead, they shall be organized by error category.

Geometry
Floating objects, Broken packaging, Impossible perspective, Warped bottles, Incorrect proportions

Materials
Plastic-looking glass, Rubber reflections, Incorrect foil, Fake paper, Artificial oil

Photography
Overexposure, HDR, Lens flare, Heavy bloom, Artificial vignette, Excessive bokeh, Neon lighting

Brand
Perfume styling, Cosmetic styling, Medicine bottle appearance, Placeholder labels, Hallucinated logos, Unreadable typography

Composition
Crowded layouts, Confusing hierarchy, Unbalanced whitespace, Distracting props, Competing focal points

The Creative System shall include only the negative constraints relevant to the current task.

↓

↓
Pass 6: Typography verification
↓

↓

Each pass refines the previous result.

CHAPTER 7
SELF-CRITIQUE ENGINE

After rendering, the Creative System shall evaluate the image against the Creative Operating System.

Questions include:
Is the business objective obvious?
Does the render protect the brand?
Is the packaging accurate?
Is the typography readable?
Are materials physically believable?
Does lighting reinforce premium perception?
Is composition balanced?
Is the render manufacturable?
Is the image suitable for its intended platform?

Each category shall receive a score from 1 to 10.
Any category scoring below the defined approval threshold shall trigger refinement rather than publication.

CHAPTER 8
ITERATIVE IMPROVEMENT LOOP

Every revision shall preserve successful elements while addressing identified deficiencies.
The system shall avoid regenerating the entire image when a localized refinement is sufficient.

Typical refinement sequence:
Correct composition.
Refine lighting.
Improve material rendering.
Correct typography.
Validate colour.
Perform final quality review.

This minimizes unnecessary variation and maintains consistency across iterations.

CHAPTER 9
FUTURE MODEL INTEGRATION

When a new rendering model becomes available:
Create a capability profile.
Benchmark against existing quality standards.
Identify strengths and limitations.
Develop a translation layer from the universal visual specification.
Validate outputs using the Quality Control Manual.
Approve only after meeting the brand's minimum quality threshold.

The Creative System shall evolve without rewriting its core principles.

CHAPTER 10

Before any commercial render is approved, the Creative System shall confirm:
✓ Business objective defined
✓ Customer objective defined
✓ Correct brand context loaded
✓ Correct product information loaded
✓ Packaging validated
✓ Colour system applied
✓ Typography system applied
✓ Photography standards applied
✓ Prompt optimized
✓ Model selected appropriately
✓ Output reviewed
✓ Quality approved

Only after all criteria are satisfied shall the render be considered production-ready.

FINAL PRINCIPLE

The purpose of AI is not to replace creative thinking.
The purpose of AI is to execute creative thinking with speed, consistency, and precision.

The enduring value of the AYURDHARA DIVYA SHAKTI Creative Operating System lies not in any specific prompt, rendering model, or technology.
Its value lies in the disciplined reasoning that transforms business strategy into trusted, premium visual communication.

As rendering technologies evolve, this system shall continue to guide every visual asset with the same principles of clarity, authenticity, craftsmanship, and commercial excellence.
