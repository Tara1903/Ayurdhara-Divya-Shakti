# SECTION 15: CREATIVE PRESENTATION & IMAGE GENERATION STANDARD

*This standard replaces all previous image generation workflows. Every AYURDHARA image is a world-class luxury presentation, rivaling agencies like Pentagram or Landor.*

## THE MANDATE
Never generate only a bottle. Never generate only a label. Never generate only a mockup.
**Generate a COMPLETE PRODUCT DESIGN PRESENTATION.**

---

## DELIVERABLE A: PRODUCTION PACKAGE
Every approved product must include all assets required for manufacturing:
1. **Front Label:** Actual size, production-ready, typography finalized.
2. **Back Label:** Ingredients, directions, warnings, barcode, QR code, batch, expiry, legal info.
3. **Outer Box:** Front, back, sides, top, bottom, perspective view.
4. **Bottle Mockup:** Front, 45-degree, close-up.
5. **Print Specifications:** Paper, material, foil, emboss, deboss, Spot UV, lamination, CMYK values.

---

## DELIVERABLE B: CREATIVE PRESENTATION BOARD
The master presentation for internal approval, structured in an editorial, museum-like layout (Apple/Aesop/Byredo aesthetic):

- **SECTION 1: Hero Product.** Large photorealistic render. Highest visual priority. Luxury lighting.
- **SECTION 2: Brand Identity.** Logo, Brand Name, Collection, Product Name, Variant, Luxury positioning.
- **SECTION 3: Product Story.** Purpose, Customer, Emotional experience, one short paragraph.
- **SECTION 4: Key Benefits.** Premium icons (Restores Balance, 100% Natural, etc.).
- **SECTION 5: Ingredient Highlights.** Hero Botanical, supporting ingredients, botanical names.
- **SECTION 6: Lifestyle Photography.** Category-specific environment (e.g., Kids = Soft cotton, Men = Dark slate).
- **SECTION 7: Front Label.** Actual size. Explain typography, hierarchy, margins.
- **SECTION 8: Back Label.** Actual size. All regulatory zones included.
- **SECTION 9: Outer Box.** Luxury 3D render.
- **SECTION 10: Botanical Illustration.** Zoomed detail. Explain opacity, interaction with typography.
- **SECTION 11: Material Specifications.** Visual display of matte label, cotton texture, gold foil, amber glass.
- **SECTION 12: Color System.** Display primary, secondary, accent colors with HEX values.
- **SECTION 13: Typography.** Hierarchy of Cinzel, Montserrat, Poppins.
- **SECTION 14: Print Notes.** Safe Area, Bleed, Foil/Emboss areas, Scan zones.
- **SECTION 15: Luxury Details.** Highlight paper grain, reflections, glass thickness.
- **SECTION 16: Marketing Render.** Instagram/Website quality hero composition.
- **SECTION 17: Creative Director Notes.** Packaging rationale, shelf impact, customer psychology.

---

## QUALITY GATE
Before delivery, ask:
1. Would Pentagram present this?
2. Would Landor approve this?
3. Would it belong on Behance?
4. Would a luxury wellness client be impressed?
5. Would a manufacturer understand the specs?
6. Are there zero AI artifacts?

*If any answer is NO, redesign internally.*
