# Purpose

ADS-BRAND-BIBLE is the master source of truth for the AYURDHARA DIVYA SHAKTI brand.

It defines the identity, philosophy, personality, positioning, tone, visual language, and long-term direction of the brand.

All creative work must align with this document.

# Brand Story

AYURDHARA DIVYA SHAKTI was established to make nature-inspired wellness products accessible for everyday living by combining traditional wellness principles with a clean, premium, and trustworthy brand experience.

---

# Mission

To make traditional wellness practices simple, accessible, and suitable for modern lifestyles.

---

# Vision

To become one of India's most trusted wellness brands focused on natural daily self-care.

---

# Brand Promise

To provide safe, authentic, and thoughtfully crafted wellness products that inspire healthy daily routines through nature-inspired formulations, premium quality, and honest practices.
