# SECTION 1
# PURPOSE

Typography is the voice of a silent package.
Before a customer reads the message, they perceive the typography.

Typography communicates:
Authority
Trust
Precision
Professionalism
Luxury
Calmness
Craftsmanship

This document establishes the permanent typography system for AYURDHARA DIVYA SHAKTI.

---

# SECTION 2
# TYPOGRAPHY PHILOSOPHY

Typography exists to communicate. Not decorate.
Beautiful typography is invisible.
The customer should remember the message, not the font.
Every typographic decision shall increase understanding.

---

# SECTION 3
# TYPOGRAPHIC PERSONALITY

The typography system shall communicate:
Modern Ayurveda
Scientific Confidence
Traditional Wisdom
Minimal Luxury
Human Warmth
Timeless Design
Premium Manufacturing
Visual Calmness

Typography shall never feel aggressive or fashionable.
It shall feel enduring.

---

# SECTION 4
# TYPOGRAPHY HIERARCHY

Every layout shall contain a clear hierarchy.

Level 1: Brand Name
↓
Level 2: Product Name
↓
Level 3: Product Category
↓
Level 4: Primary Benefit
↓
Level 5: Supporting Description
↓
Level 6: Ingredients
↓
Level 7: Directions
↓
Level 8: Legal Information

The customer shall never wonder what to read first.

---

# SECTION 5
# READING FLOW

The eye naturally follows a structured path.

Typography shall support:
Top
↓
Primary Focus
↓
Secondary Focus
↓
Supporting Information
↓
Action
↓
Legal Information

Reading shall feel effortless.

---

# SECTION 6
# FONT PAIRING

The system shall use no more than two primary typefaces within a single design.

Primary Typeface: Brand identity and headings.
Secondary Typeface: Supporting information and body text.

Decorative fonts are prohibited.
Novelty fonts are prohibited.
Script fonts shall only be used for verified heritage applications.

---

# SECTION 7
# TYPOGRAPHIC CONTRAST

Contrast shall create structure.
Contrast may be achieved through:
Weight
Size
Spacing
Case
Colour
Position

Contrast shall never rely on unnecessary effects.

---

# SECTION 8
# LETTER SPACING

Tracking shall improve readability.
Never compress typography to fit content.
Never excessively expand typography for decoration.
Spacing shall feel balanced at every viewing distance.

---

# SECTION 9
# KERNING

Every headline shall appear optically balanced.
Letter relationships shall feel intentional.
Mechanical spacing without optical correction is discouraged.
Typography shall appear professionally typeset.

---

# SECTION 10
# LINE HEIGHT

Line spacing shall improve reading rhythm.
Crowded text reduces trust.
Excessive spacing reduces continuity.
Every paragraph shall breathe naturally.

---

# SECTION 11
# LINE LENGTH

Body copy shall remain comfortable to read.
Excessively long lines reduce comprehension.
Very short lines interrupt rhythm.
Typography shall support continuous eye movement.

---

# SECTION 12
# ALIGNMENT

Preferred alignment:
Left
or
Optically Centered

Mixed alignments shall only be used when serving a documented communication purpose.
Random alignment changes are prohibited.

---

# SECTION 13
# CAPITALIZATION

Capitalization communicates tone.

Sentence Case
↓
Natural, Friendly, Readable

Title Case
↓
Premium, Editorial, Professional

ALL CAPS
↓
Use only for: Short Labels, Category Names, Small Functional Elements

Long passages shall never appear in all capitals.

---

# SECTION 14
# NUMBERS

Numerical information shall remain highly legible.

Examples:
Volume
Batch
Expiry
Manufacturing
Price
Dimensions

Numbers shall never compete with product identity.

---

# SECTION 15
# MULTI-LANGUAGE SUPPORT

Where multiple languages are required:

Typography shall respect:
English
Hindi
Sanskrit
Future international languages

Translations shall never appear as visual afterthoughts.

# SECTION 18
# ACCESSIBILITY

Typography shall remain readable for a broad audience.

Avoid:
Tiny text
Poor contrast
Decorative distortion
Complex letterforms

Accessibility improves customer trust.

---

# SECTION 19
# PROHIBITED TYPOGRAPHY

The following are prohibited unless explicitly approved:
3D Text
Bevel Effects
Drop Shadows
Neon Typography
Glow Effects
Comic Fonts
Handwritten Novelty Fonts
Distorted Letterforms
Extreme Condensed Fonts
Extreme Expanded Fonts
Gradient Typography

Typography shall never compete with the product.
