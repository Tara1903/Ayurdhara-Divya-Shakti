# Print Specifications

- **Print Method:** CMYK High-Definition Digital Print
- **DPI:** 300 DPI minimum
- **Bleed & Margins:** Must maintain proper bleed and safe margins to ensure perfect alignment and readability.
- **Color Accuracy:** Must adhere strictly to CMYK profiles for Primary Green, Cream, and Gold Accents to prevent variations in print.
- **Barcodes/QRs:** Ensure high contrast for scannability, printed sharply in black.
